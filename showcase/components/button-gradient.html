Known Figma characteristics reproduced

  • GB-3 Size 24 Focus label is Label XSmall Semibold 12/17; other size-24 states are 12/18.

  • GB-2 Gradient angle depends on the frame aspect ratio; exact at the documented width.
  • GB-4 / B9 Hover fill and all shadows are raw values (no variables / effect styles).
  • A14 / D-22 Label contrast against the light gradient end 3.89:1.
