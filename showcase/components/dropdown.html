Menu variants (8 — Theme=Light, Footer=None; static, open)

Theme = Dark and Footer (R6 — 60 variants; docs/figma-audit/r6-0-evidence.md)

Dark is Figma's Theme=Dark: set data-annachy-theme="dark" on the menu (or an ancestor). The Brand Dark mode values come from src/tokens/themes/dark.css; Dark Selected uses Blue/500 with a white label. The Footer holds an R3 Primary Button (size 40 Desktop / 48 Mobile, label typography overridden as in Figma). With a footer, choices are staged: they are committed — and annachy-change fires — only on “Apply selection”; Escape, outside click or Tab-out discard them.
